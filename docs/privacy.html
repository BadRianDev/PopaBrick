Privacy policy

Pop a Brick · last updated 3 November 2026

The short version

Pop a Brick is developed and published by BadRianDev. The game does not send your game profile or personal information to us. It has no player accounts, analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs. Progress and preferences stay on your TV. Store purchases, support requests and visits to these pages involve separate services, explained below.

What the game stores on your TV

The game uses local browser storage under nbb.profile.v1 to remember:

  • highScore: your high score;
  • maxLevel: the highest level reached;
  • totalBricks: total bricks broken;
  • sound: your sound preference;
  • controlMode: automatic, pointer or button controls;
  • purchased: a legacy compatibility flag set locally for the paid game, not a payment record.

This profile contains no name, email address, account or payment details. The game does not upload or sync it to a server.

The profile remains while your TV retains this storage. Clearing app storage, where supported, or resetting the TV may remove it. We cannot recover removed progress. Whether uninstalling clears storage depends on the TV and webOS; uninstalling alone is not guaranteed to erase it.

Buying the game

Pop a Brick is paid for up front through the LG Content Store. All 50 levels are included; reaching later levels depends on gameplay progress. There are no in-app purchases, unlock transactions, subscriptions or recurring charges in the game.

LG handles store purchases and the account and payment information needed for them under its own terms and privacy policy. In paid mode the game does not collect payment details or request an in-app purchase confirmation from LG. Consult the privacy policy shown by LG for your region, and contact LG Customer Support for store, account, billing or refund questions.

Email support

Email badriandev@gmail.com for private support or privacy enquiries. We receive your address, message and any attachments and use them to respond and investigate problems. We do not publish your email as a GitHub issue. Email providers process messages to deliver them; our Gmail inbox is provided by Google under Google's privacy policy.

Send only what is needed, such as TV model, webOS version, game version and what happened. Do not send passwords, card details or unnecessary account or order information. Check photos and videos for personal information.

Public GitHub support

You can also use our issue forms. Your GitHub username, issue text, comments and attachments are publicly visible. We use reports to answer questions, investigate bugs and improve the game. GitHub processes information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Do not post names, addresses, payment details, LG account emails or order numbers publicly. Use email for private enquiries.

You can edit your content using GitHub's available controls. Contact us about personal information you want removed. Closing an issue does not delete it; copies and GitHub's own records may remain outside our control.

These support pages

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. GitHub may process technical information, such as IP addresses and browser information, to provide and secure hosting, as described in its privacy statement. External support links do not upload the game's local profile.

Retention and your rights

We retain support correspondence as needed to handle requests, follow up on problems and meet applicable legal obligations. Public issues may remain as a record of bugs and answers. LG, GitHub and email providers have their own retention practices, which we do not control.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to its use, request portability, or complain to a data protection authority. Email us about support records we manage. We will consider requests under applicable law; we cannot guarantee removal of records controlled by other services. Contact those services for their account, payment or hosting records.

Children

The game has no player accounts or personal-data collection features, including for children. Children should ask a parent or guardian to handle purchases and support requests, and should not post personal information in public issues.

Contact and changes

Contact BadRianDev at badriandev@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests. Changes to this policy will appear here with a new last-updated date.
