Privacy notice

Pop a Brick · last updated 27 October 2026

The short version

Pop a Brick does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. Everything the game remembers stays on your television.

What is stored on your TV

The game saves a single entry in the TV's local browser storage under the key nbb.profile.v1. It contains:

  • the highest level you have reached and your high score;
  • the total number of bricks you have broken;
  • your sound on/off preference;
  • a flag recording whether the full game has been unlocked.

None of this identifies you. It never leaves the TV, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or reset the television.

Purchases

The one-off unlock is sold and processed by the LG Content Store. We never see or receive your name, address or payment details — we only receive confirmation from LG that an entitlement exists for the device. LG's own privacy policy governs the payment itself, and LG acts as the data controller for that transaction.

Support requests

Support is handled through issue forms on our public GitHub repository. If you submit one, the information you type and your GitHub username become publicly visible on github.com, and GitHub processes that data under GitHub's own privacy statement.

Please do not put personal information — your name, address, payment details or LG account email — into a public issue. We do not need it, and we will ask you to edit it out if you do.

You can delete or edit your own issue content on GitHub at any time, and you may ask us to close and remove a report by commenting on it.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children in a way that involves any data collection, because the game collects no data.

Your rights

Because the game holds no personal data about you, there is normally nothing to access, correct or erase. For anything you have posted in a support issue, you control that content directly through your GitHub account.

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

Contact

Privacy questions can be raised through the support issue form.

If this notice changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
