You will need a free GitHub account to submit a form. Existing reports are visible at github.com/BadRianDev/popabrick/issues — please search there first, in case your question has already been answered.

What to include in a bug report

The form will ask for these; having them to hand makes it quicker:

  • TV model — Settings → Support → TV Information (e.g. OLED55C34LA)
  • webOS version — same screen
  • Game version — shown on the About & Support screen in the game
  • The level you were on and what you were doing

A photo or short phone video of the TV screen helps a great deal.

Issues and attachments are public. Do not post names, addresses, payment details, LG account emails or order numbers. Check photos and videos before sharing them.

Frequently asked questions

How do I control the paddle?

Point the Magic Remote left or right and the paddle follows the cursor. If you are not using the pointer, the RED button moves left and the BLUE button moves right. The D-pad arrows work too. Press OK to launch the ball and to fire the guns.

How much does it cost?

The game is paid for up front through the LG Content Store, with all 50 levels included. Later levels are reached through gameplay progress. There is no subscription or in-app purchase. Check the current listing for price and availability in your region.

I paid but the game will not install

Check that the TV is online and signed in to the LG account used for purchase, then check the download or installation in the LG Content Store. For store, account or billing problems, contact LG Customer Support for your country. If the installed game itself will not start, open a purchase or download problem report.

What happens to my progress if I reset the TV?

Progress, scores and settings are stored locally on the TV, not backed up on a server. Resetting the TV may remove this data, and we cannot recover it. Store purchases are handled separately by LG; contact LG for help accessing a previous purchase.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are made through the LG Content Store, so refunds follow LG's own process — contact LG Customer Support for your country. See the note on the right of withdrawal in our terms.

Is the game safe for photosensitive viewers?

Pop a Brick uses bright colours and particle effects but no deliberate strobing. If you or anyone in your household has a photosensitive condition, play in a well-lit room and take regular breaks.
