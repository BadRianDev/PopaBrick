# Support — Pop a Brick

All support goes through the issue forms in this repository.

**[→ Open a support request](https://github.com/BadRianDev/popabrick/issues/new/choose)**

Choose the form that fits:

- **Bug report** — something in the game is broken or behaves unexpectedly
- **Purchase or unlock problem** — you bought the full game but levels are still locked
- **Feedback or idea** — suggestions and improvements

Please [search existing issues](https://github.com/BadRianDev/popabrick/issues) first.

The [support pages](https://badriandev.github.io/popabrick/) answer the most common questions
about controls, free levels, purchases and privacy.

## Before you report a bug

| Detail | Where to find it |
| --- | --- |
| TV model | Settings → Support → TV Information (e.g. OLED55C34LA) |
| webOS version | Same screen |
| Game version | **ABOUT & SUPPORT** screen in the game |
| What happened | The level, and what you were doing |

A photo or short phone video of the TV screen helps a lot.

## Privacy when reporting

Issues in this repository are **public**. Do not post your name, address, payment details,
LG account email or order numbers — we do not need them, and we will ask you to remove them
if you do. See the [privacy notice](https://badriandev.github.io/popabrick/privacy.html).

## Purchases and refunds

The unlock is sold and billed by the **LG Content Store**, not by us, and we never see your
payment details.

- **Levels still locked after paying:** open the store screen in the game and select the
  unlock again — an existing purchase is restored at no extra charge. Check the TV is online
  and signed in to the LG account used for the purchase.
- **Refunds:** these follow LG's process. Contact LG Customer Support for your country.

## Scope

Support is provided on a best-efforts basis. Reports that cannot be reproduced, or that are
about television hardware or LG account problems rather than the game, may be closed. This
does not affect your statutory consumer rights.
