# Support — Pop a Brick

Use the public issue forms for bug reports and ideas, or email
**[badriandev@gmail.com](mailto:badriandev@gmail.com)** for private support or privacy questions.

**[→ Open a support request](https://github.com/BadRianDev/popabrick/issues/new/choose)**

Choose the form that fits:

- **Bug report** — something in the game is broken or behaves unexpectedly
- **Purchase or download problem** — you bought the game but it will not download, install or start
- **Feedback or idea** — suggestions and improvements

Please [search existing issues](https://github.com/BadRianDev/popabrick/issues) first.

The [support pages](https://badriandev.github.io/PopaBrick/) answer the most common questions
about controls, purchases and privacy.

## Before you report a bug

| Detail | Where to find it |
| --- | --- |
| TV model | Settings → Support → TV Information (e.g. OLED55C34LA) |
| webOS version | Same screen |
| Game version | **ABOUT & SUPPORT** screen in the game |
| What happened | The level, and what you were doing |

A photo or short phone video of the TV screen helps a lot.

## Privacy when reporting

Issues in this repository are **public**. Do not post your name, address, payment details,
LG account email or order numbers — we do not need them, and we will ask you to remove them
if you do. See the [privacy policy](https://badriandev.github.io/PopaBrick/privacy.html).

Email is not a public issue, but we receive your address, message and attachments to handle
your request. Please send only what is needed and check attachments for personal information.

## Purchases and refunds

The game is paid for up front through the **LG Content Store**, which handles billing.
All 50 levels are included; reaching later levels depends on gameplay progress. There are
no in-app purchases, unlock transactions or subscriptions. The game does not collect
payment details. Check the current listing for price and availability in your region.

- **Paid but the game will not install:** check the TV is online and signed in to the
  LG account used for the purchase, then check the download or installation in the Content
  Store. Contact LG Customer Support for store, billing or account problems. If the installed
  game itself will not start, send us a support request.
- **Refunds:** these follow LG's process. Contact LG Customer Support for your country.

## Scope

Support is provided on a best-efforts basis. Reports that cannot be reproduced, or that are
about television hardware or LG account problems rather than the game, may be closed. This
does not affect your statutory consumer rights.
